Question 1: PCA by Hand

Consider the following dataset, containing 5 examples with 2 features each: (−2, −1), (−1, 0), (0, 1), (1, 2), (2, 3). Recall that with PCA we usually assume that the PCs are normalized ($\|w\| = 1$), we need to center the data before we apply PCA, and that the direction of the first PC is the one that minimizes the orthogonal distance to all data points.

Part 1: First Principal Component

What is the first principal component?

Step 1: Calculate the Mean Vector

$$m_x = \frac{1}{5}\left(\begin{bmatrix}-2\\-1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}-1\\0\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}2\\3\end{bmatrix}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\begin{bmatrix}0\\5\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}$$

Step 2: Covariance Matrix

$$E[xx^T] = \frac{1}{5}\left(\begin{bmatrix}-2\\-1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}-2 & -1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}-1\\0\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}2\\3\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}2 & 3\end{bmatrix}\right)$$ $$= \frac{1}{5}\left(\begin{bmatrix}4 & 2\\2 & 1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}1 & 0\\0 & 0\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0 & 0\\0 & 1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\\2 & 4\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}4 & 6\\6 & 9\end{bmatrix}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\begin{bmatrix}10 & 10\\10 & 15\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 2\\2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$$ $$E[m_x m_x^T] = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}0 & 0\\0 & 1\end{bmatrix}$$ $$C_x = E[xx^T] - E[m_x m_x^T] = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 2\\2 & 2\end{bmatrix}$$

Step 3: Find Eigenvalues

$$\det(C_x - \lambda I) = \begin{vmatrix}2-\lambda & 2\\2 & 2-\lambda\end{vmatrix} = (2-\lambda)^2 - 4 = 0$$ $$4 - 4\lambda + \lambda^2 - 4 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \lambda(\lambda - 4) = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \lambda = 0,\ 4$$

Step 4: Find Eigenvectors

We know $C_y = \text{diag}(4, 0)$, so $\text{Var}(y_1) = 4$. Investigate this:

$$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 2\\2 & 2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}w_1\\w_2\end{bmatrix} = 4\begin{bmatrix}w_1\\w_2\end{bmatrix} \;\Rightarrow\; \begin{cases}2w_1 + 2w_2 = 4w_1\\ 2w_1 + 2w_2 = 4w_2\end{cases} \;\Rightarrow\; w_1 = w_2$$

Normalize:

$$\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix} \;\rightarrow\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$$

Answer

First principal component:

$$w_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$$

Part 2: Reconstruction Error of (3, 3)

What is the (L2-norm) reconstruction error of the point (3,3)? (Show your work.)

Step 1: Project to the Principal Component

Center the point:

$$x_c = \begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}3\\2\end{bmatrix}$$

Projection:

$$y_1 = \begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\end{bmatrix} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}}$$

Step 2: Reconstruct $x$

$$\hat{x} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} + \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}5/2\\5/2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}5/2\\7/2\end{bmatrix}$$

Step 3: Calculate Error

$$\|x - \hat{x}\|_2 = \left\|\begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}5/2\\7/2\end{bmatrix}\right\|_2 = \sqrt{\left(3 - \tfrac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(3 - \tfrac{7}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.7071$$

Answer

Reconstruction error of (3, 3): $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.7071$

Part 3: Reconstruction Error of (3, 4)

What is the (L2-norm) reconstruction error of the point (3,4)? (Show your work.)

Step 1: Project to the Principal Component

$$x_c = \begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix}$$ $$y_1 = \begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{2}}$$

Step 2: Reconstruct $x$

$$\hat{x} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} + \frac{6}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix}$$

Step 3: Calculate Error

$$\|x - \hat{x}\|_2 = \left\|\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix}\right\|_2 = 0$$

Answer

Reconstruction error of (3, 4): $0$

The point (3, 4) lies exactly on the line through the mean in the direction of the first PC ($x_2 = x_1 + 1$), so it is reconstructed perfectly.
