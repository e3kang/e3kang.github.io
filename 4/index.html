Comment: This review mixes both positive and negative words. The reviewer describes the film as "really enjoyed", and uses a lot of negative words for the DVD format, not the movie (eg. "hate" and "botched"). The repeated question marks (typically associated with negative reviews) also add up the log likelihood each time.

Summary

The confident errors fall into a few groups:

  • Right connotation, wrong context: Many of these reviews use negative words aimed at something other than the product, which doesn't evaluate properly in a bag of words.
  • Language indirectly associated with positive/negative connotation: Things like antivirus names, question marks (?), and "botched" are more present in negative reviews, even though they can have rather neutral intent.
  • Complex text: The review genuinely seems the opposite of the provided label.
